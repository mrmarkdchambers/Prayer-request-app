## Description (Apple 4000 max · Google 4000 max)

```
Intercede is a free, private prayer list. Keep track of what you're praying for, pray through a few requests each day, pray together with your small group, and look back on how God has answered.

PRAY EVERY DAY
• Add requests in seconds: "Sarah: healing after surgery #family"
• Paste a whole list at once
• A daily prayer time brings up a few requests at a time, rotating so nothing is forgotten
• Set requests to come up every day, on certain weekdays, or monthly
• Post updates as things change, and set follow-up dates to check in

REMEMBER WHAT GOD HAS DONE
• Mark prayers answered and write down how
• Add praise reports for anything you're thankful for
• Look back on a timeline of answered prayers and praise, month by month

PRAY TOGETHER
• Start a group for your small group, family or friends and invite people with a link or QR code
• Choose for each request whether it's just for you or shared with a group
• Tap "I prayed" so friends know they're being prayed for, and "Praise God" to celebrate answers
• Get a notification when someone prays for you or your group shares news

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no tracking, no selling your data
• Works without an account; sign in only if you want sync and groups
• Your prayers are visible only to you, and shared requests only to the group you choose
• Delete your account and everything in it at any time

ALSO
• Daily reminder at the time you choose
• Works offline
• Back up or export your list any time
• Dark mode

Intercede is free, with every feature included.
```
